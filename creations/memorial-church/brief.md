# Memorial Church

A Snapwright creation brief.

## 1. What it is
Stanford's Memorial Church seen from the Main Quad: a sandstone Romanesque church with a tall
gabled front covered in a colourful mosaic (the Sermon on the Mount) under a cross, a big
arched rose window with three small arched windows each side, a three-arched entrance porch
with gold mosaic spandrels, low red-tile-roofed arcades running left and right, and round
drum towers just visible behind.

## 2. Rights
A real place (a building), fine to model. Name it "Memorial Church"; no university logos or
marks in the book or files. References (a generated front elevation and the photos it was made from) are not in the repo; mosaic_keys.json holds what the design needs.

## 3. Size and budget
The Quad frontage on a small plaza: about 43 x 26 cm and 27 cm to the top of the cross,
roughly 2,500-3,500 parts. Diorama: a paved plaza with the round paving circle.

## 4. Who builds it
Adult. Smooth tiled finish.

## 5. Must-read features
1. The gable mosaic: a sideways (upright) mosaic panel made from the photo, 28 studs wide.
2. The rose window and the small arched windows (dark blue).
3. The three arched portals with gold mosaic spandrels.
4. The red-tile arcades either side (3 bays each, 3-stud arches on round columns).
5. The cross on the apex; the drum towers behind.

## 6. Colours
Sandstone tan (walls), dark tan (trim, bands, column bases), dark orange (tile roofs), dark
blue (windows), reddish brown (doors), pearl gold / bright light orange (mosaic gold), the
mosaic's own colours from the photo; plaza tan and dark tan paving with a red and
sand-blue-grey circle.

## 7. Proportions (front view, from reference/view_front.png)
Facade block 28 studs wide. Heights in plates from the plaza: portal storey 0-23 (arch
crowns at 16), carved band 23-27, window storey 27-50, gable eaves at 50, apex 75, cross top
85. Arcades: eaves at 21, ridge 27. Drum towers: walls to 46, roofs to 54.
Portals: 2 + 6 + 3 + 6 + 3 + 6 + 2 studs (piers and arches).

## 8. Structure
Everything stands on a 2-plate plaza base. The gable mosaic is a sideways panel clipped onto
side studs on the gable wall; the wall behind it must be solid on its anchor rows. The
arcade roofs span columns: keep the arches' lintels tied into the back wall.

## 9. Outputs
Instruction book (PDF), 3D viewer, parts lists (BrickLink / Rebrickable), LDraw, build video.

## 10. Open questions
- Arcade length: 3 bays each side (the real one has more); 4 would make it about 50 cm wide.
